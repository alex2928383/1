ABOUT EAGLEVOICE

EagleVoice is an independent, student-led feedback initiative created to help bridge the gap between the student body and campus leadership.

As a student representative, I use this platform to gather candid, unfiltered campus perspectives. Recurring themes and aggregated insights are analyzed to help inform student advocacy discussions, committee work, and campus feedback initiatives.

> YOUR PRIVACY & USAGE

To ensure uncompromised honesty, this portal is completely anonymous. We do not collect names, emails, or student IDs. Submissions are routed directly to an encrypted database to analyze high-level trends without ever compromising your identity.

> NOTICE

EagleVoice is an independent, student-run platform and is not an official university department or emergency service. It is not monitored 24/7. For campus emergencies, safety concerns, Title IX reports, or formal university grievances, please contact official campus resources directly.

Legal & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

1. Data Collection & Anonymity

EagleVoice does not require or collect names, email addresses, Student IDs, or account registrations. When you submit feedback, we collect:

  • The text content you provide
  • Basic technical data (timestamps and system identifiers)
  • Limited technical info processed by our provider (Supabase) for security

2. How Data Is Stored

Submissions are securely stored using Supabase. While reasonable safeguards are used, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

3. Purpose of Data Use

Feedback is used exclusively for student advocacy, including identifying concerns and sharing aggregated insights with university stakeholders like the PSAC. No data is sold for advertising.

4. Website Analytics

To better understand how the community uses EagleVoice and to improve the platform, we use Google Analytics. This service collects non-personally identifiable information such as page views, session duration, and general device information.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, Student ID, or email address through these tools. Google Analytics may process certain technical identifiers such as anonymized IP addresses per their own privacy policy. IP anonymization is enabled on this platform.

You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Anonymity Notice

EagleVoice is designed for anonymity. However, true anonymity cannot be absolutely guaranteed. Avoid including personal details if anonymity is your priority.

6. Informal Platform Disclaimer

EagleVoice is an independent initiative and not an official UNT reporting system. Submissions do not constitute formal grievances or Title IX reports.

7. User Conduct

Users agree not to submit content that is threatening, harassing, knowingly false, or in violation of university conduct policies.

8. Limitation of Liability

The platform is provided "as is." The developer is not responsible for data loss, service interruptions, or outcomes resulting from the use of this tool.
